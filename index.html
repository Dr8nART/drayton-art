The Idea

Two views of one home.

For many Southport homes, the most interesting story is the relationship between the house people know today and the house as it may have appeared years—or generations—earlier.

8 × 12 inch engraved tile

Historic Home Portrait

An artistic rendering of an earlier appearance of your home. Whenever possible, I work from old family photographs or other visual evidence. When photographs do not exist, I can develop a carefully considered AI-assisted reconstruction using the home's architecture, documented changes, and other available references.

4 × 6 inch engraved tile

Present-Day Home Portrait

A companion engraving of the house as it looks now. I photograph the home myself so the finished image can be composed specifically for engraving rather than being limited by an existing snapshot.

Before anything is engraved, you see the proposed artwork. I normally prepare one carefully chosen version for approval and include one reasonable revision for factual or architectural corrections. This keeps the process personal and manageable while still giving you a chance to catch an important detail.

About Drayton

Made here, for homes here.

I am Drayton Hawthorne. I have lived and worked in this area for many years and have developed a particular interest in the buildings, stories, and visual history of Southport.

My tile work combines a developed fine-line engraving style with modern digital tools. For historic reconstructions, I use AI as a drawing and visualization aid—not as a substitute for evidence. Old photographs, architectural features, homeowner knowledge, and documented changes remain the most important guides.

The finished artwork is intended to be both personal and attractive: something that records the house as it stands today while also giving a thoughtful glimpse of an earlier chapter in its life.
